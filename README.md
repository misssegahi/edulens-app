EduLens AI 📚🤖

EduLens AI is an educational AI tool that helps students understand study material more easily. Students can upload an image of their study material and get AI-powered explanations, summaries, and quick questions.

✨ Features

📷 Upload study material as an image

🧠 AI-powered visual explanations

📝 Generate simple summaries

❓ Generate quick questions for revision

🎓 Designed for students and learning

🎯 Purpose

The goal of EduLens AI is to make studying more interactive and easier to understand by turning study material into clear explanations and useful revision content.

🚀 How to Use

Open EduLens AI.

Upload an image of your study material.

Let the AI analyze the material.

Read the generated explanation and summary.

Use the quick questions to revise what you learned.

💡 Why EduLens?

Traditional study methods can sometimes make difficult topics confusing. EduLens AI uses artificial intelligence to transform study material into simpler, more understandable learning content.

🛠️ Project

EduLens AI was created as an AI-based educational project focused on making learning more accessible, interactive, and student-friendly.
